What we repair

Most calls fall into one of these. If yours doesn't, call anyway. If we can't fix it, we'll tell you who can.

Pumps and motors

Pumps that hum and won't start, trip the breaker, leak at the shaft seal or keep losing prime. We rebuild or replace, and we'll tell you when a variable-speed pump makes more sense than another repair on an old single-speed.

Heaters

Gas heaters and heat pumps from Raypak, Pentair, Hayward and Jandy. Ignition failures, pressure switches, bad sensors, and heat exchangers scaled up by our hard water.

Filters

Cartridge, DE and sand filters. Cracked tanks, worn multiport valves, and pressure that climbs faster than it should between cleanings.

Leak detection and repair

If the pool loses more than about a quarter inch a day, it's not evaporation. We pressure-test the plumbing and dye-test the shell, lights and fittings, then fix what we find.

Salt systems

Hayward AquaRite, Pentair IntelliChlor and Jandy AquaPure. Cells that read low after you've added salt, flow sensors, and control boards.

Automation and pool lights

Control panels that drop their schedules, apps that lose the pool, and pool or spa lights that won't come on. We repair LED and incandescent fixtures and the wiring behind them.

Green pool cleanup

Neglected or storm-hit pools brought back to clear water. We check the equipment while we're at it, since a green pool usually means something stopped running.

Equipment installs

New pumps, heaters, filters and salt systems, sized for the pool. We install what we repair, so there's one company to call afterward.

The price comes before the work

The tech diagnoses first and quotes second. You approve the number, then we start. If something else turns up once we're in there, we stop and call you.

We also tell you when a repair isn't worth it. A twelve-year-old single-speed pump with a bad motor is usually a replacement, not a rebuild, and we'll say so.

We know Conejo Valley water

Water here is hard. That scale shows up on salt cells, heater exchangers and tile long before it shows up on your bill. Our techs work on it every day, so repairs account for it and last longer.

If you want someone to keep an eye on the pool after it's fixed, we run weekly service routes through Thousand Oaks and Newbury Park too.

Common questions

Do you repair pools you don't service weekly?

Yes. Repair calls are open to any pool owner in our service area. Plenty of our repair customers have a different weekly service company or clean the pool themselves.

How soon can someone come out?

Call and we'll give you a real date. Pumps and green pools go first in summer, because the water turns fast. Tell us if the pool is already turning.

Do you charge for the diagnosis?

There's a service call fee for the visit and the diagnosis. If you go ahead with the repair, we tell you up front how that applies. You'll have the full price before any work starts.
