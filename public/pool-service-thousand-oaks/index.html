What a visit includes

  • Test the water and balance chlorine, pH, alkalinity, calcium and stabilizer
  • Skim the surface and brush the walls, steps and tile line
  • Empty the skimmer and pump baskets, and the cleaner bag
  • Check the pump, filter pressure, chlorinator or salt cell, and the heater
  • Note anything wearing out before it fails, with a price if it needs attention
  • Vacuum when the pool needs it

Filter cleanings are scheduled on top of the weekly visit, every few months depending on the pool. We'll tell you when one is due.

Why repair techs on the route matter

The person cleaning your pool each week is the one who notices the pump seal weeping, the filter pressure creeping up, or the heater taking longer than it used to. On our routes that's reported back the same day and a repair tech handles it.

That's the difference between a small part now and a motor, or a green pool, later.

Simple to deal with

Service is billed monthly and you can pay online. If something comes up you can call or text the office. Reviews mention the same thing over and over: we show up when we say we will.

Chemical-only service is available if you'd rather do the brushing yourself. Ask when you call.

Weekly service questions

What does weekly service cost?

It depends on the pool size, the equipment and whether there's a spa. We'll quote after a look at the pool. Chemicals are included.

Do I need to be home?

No. We need gate access and a clear path to the pool and the equipment. Dogs are fine if we know about them.

What if my pool turns green?

If it happens on our watch, we fix it. If you're starting with a green pool, we clean it up first and then put it on the route.

Which areas do you cover?

Thousand Oaks, Newbury Park, Westlake Village, Oak Park, Agoura Hills, Moorpark, Simi Valley and Camarillo. Calabasas and Hidden Hills by request.
