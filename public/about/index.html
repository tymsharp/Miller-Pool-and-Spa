About Miller Pool and Spa

Mason Miller learned pool work from his father and started this company on his own, cleaning and repairing pools around Newbury Park. It grew by referral into a crew serving homeowners across Ventura and Los Angeles County, and it's been at it for more than ten years.

In 2026 Parker took over ownership and has been bringing other local pool companies under the Miller name. The trucks still roll out of Newbury Park every morning, and the job is the same as it was on day one: show up when we say we will, fix it right, and charge what we quoted.

Today the company looks after more than 700 pools on weekly service and runs repair techs across the Conejo Valley, Simi Valley and Camarillo.

How we work

The price comes before the work. The tech diagnoses first, quotes second, and you approve the number before anything is replaced.

We show up when we say we will. On weekly routes that means the same day and roughly the same time each week. On repairs it means a real date, not a window that slides.

We fix what we install. If we put in the pump, we're the ones who come back if it ever gives you trouble.

The people you'll meet

Customers mention our techs by name in reviews, which is the best kind of review. Jon and Jarett run a lot of the leak detection work. The office answers the phone during the day and calls back if you leave a message.
